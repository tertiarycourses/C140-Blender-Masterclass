## About the course

Blender is a free, open-source 3D creation suite. This course takes beginners through the full production path: reading a creative brief and setting a polygon budget, controlling the Blender interface, modelling and sculpting with clean topology, UV unwrapping and PBR materials, rigging and keyframe animation, then cameras, lighting, rendering, optimisation and export.

The eight labs are connected around one case study — a goldfish hero asset for a fish-bowl scene — so each lab builds on the last. The trainer deck focuses on concepts and production judgement; detailed click-by-click procedures live in the Learner Guide and each lab's printable `INSTRUCTIONS.pdf`.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1** - Analyse a creative brief and establish a suitable polygon budget.
- **LO2** - Develop 3D models and prototypes in Blender.
- **LO3** - Animate 3D models using suitable rigging, keyframe and presentation methods.
- **LO4** - Optimise, light, render and export 3D models for the intended use.

## Topics covered

1. **Get Started with Blender and the Creative Brief** - creative briefs as production contracts, polygon budgets, workspaces and editors, navigation, selection and transforms, objects, collections and modifiers.
2. **Develop 3D Models and Prototypes** - translating concept art into form, mesh anatomy and edge flow, Edit Mode tools, sculpting from broad forms to detail, reviewing and refining the prototype.
3. **Animate and Present the 3D Prototype** - animation principles, armatures and weights, keyframes and curves, shaders, UVs and textures, presenting the prototype for feedback.
4. **Optimise, Render and Export the Model** - cameras, purposeful lighting, render engines and settings, optimising the asset, exporting and verifying the delivery package.

## Schedule

Two days, 9:30 am to 5:30 pm, each with a single 30-minute lunch: 7.5 instructional hours a day of demonstrations, guided practice, labs and review. Day 1 covers Topics 1 and 2 (Labs 1–4); Day 2 covers Topics 3 and 4 (Labs 5–8) and closes with the capstone and course feedback. The full timed schedule is in the Lesson Plan.

## Prerequisites

- Able to operate a computer confidently.
- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/download/) 5.2 LTS or a compatible current release on Windows or macOS.
- A laptop capable of opening the supplied `.blend` files; a three-button mouse is recommended.
